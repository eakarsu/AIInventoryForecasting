# Quarantined generated gap surfaces

The `backend/routes/gap-*` files and matching `frontend/src/pages/Gap*` pages are provenance-unknown generated placeholders. They are retained for owner review but are not imported or mounted by `backend/src/index.js` and are not evidence of implemented capability. Product owners must either delete them after confirming provenance or replace them through reviewed domain workflows, tests, and authoritative adapters.

Generic OpenRouter output is restricted to explanatory assistance; it is not accepted as a forecast, replenishment approval, procurement order, or connector response. The governed workflow uses deterministic schemas and explicit human/adapter gates.
